SP-404SX/A EXTRAfirmware

Tape-style varispeed for every pad, a chromatic keyboard mode (pads and MIDI), mute groups and per-pad pan on the Roland SP-404SX and SP-404A (same firmware, works the same on both).

Release …

1Get Roland’s official update

Download SP-404SX System Update Version 1.07 from Roland’s support page and unzip it. You need the file sp404sx_up.bin inside, unchanged. SP-404A owners use this same file.

This site contains no Roland code. It only adds this project’s changes to your copy of Roland’s file.

2Make the EXTRAfirmware file

3Install it

  1. Back up your SD card. Use a card formatted by the SP-404SX/A with at least 2 MB free.
  2. Copy the downloaded sp404sx_up.bin to the root of the card (keep that exact name).
  3. Power off the SP-404SX/A, insert the card, connect the AC adaptor (not batteries) and power on.
  4. The display shows UPd and then Fin after about ten seconds. Never power off while it says UPd.
  5. Power off, take out the card and delete sp404sx_up.bin from it on your computer (otherwise it updates again at every power-on), then put the card back in the SP-404SX/A.

To check the install, hold MARK while powering on: the display shows 2.20 (stock shows 1.07). To go back to stock, install Roland’s original sp404sx_up.bin the same way.

What it adds

Varispeed

  • CTRL 3 on the TIME/BPM page sets each pad’s speed, tape style (pitch and tempo together): fully left OFF, then −12 to +7 semitones (centre = normal), fully right PTN to follow the pattern tempo live (0.25× to 4×).
  • The display shows the BPM you hear. CTRL 1 and CTRL 2 also show their value × the pad’s speed.
  • Works together with Time Modify. Moving CTRL 2 resets varispeed, the same way it resets Time Modify.

Chromatic mode

  • Play a pad and press SHIFT + TIME/BPM: its bank becomes a keyboard for it (pad 1 = its own pitch, +1 semitone per pad, empty pads too). TIME/BPM blinks while the menu is open; the main screen shows C.ON on a chromatic bank.
  • CTRL 1 sets the bank’s MIDI channel (free channels only; middle C = its own pitch), CTRL 3 the pads’ octave (−2 to +2). One note at a time per bank; several banks at once, each with its own sample and channel.
  • HOLD latches every key, MARK keeps the note’s pitch, varispeed moves the whole keyboard.
  • Works while resampling, so you can record melodies: at RDY / REC the pads and MIDI notes play, and the first note starts the recording.
  • Turn it off with CTRL 1 fully left (OFF), or hold SHIFT + TIME/BPM and press CANCEL.

Mute groups

  • Play a pad and press SHIFT + MARK on the main screen (MARK blinks): CTRL 1 puts it in mute group M 1–M10 or OFF. MARK closes the menu.
  • A pad starting stops the other playing pads of its group (choke, e.g. open and closed hi-hat), in any bank, whether started from pads, MIDI or patterns.

Pan

  • Play a pad and press SHIFT + START/END/LEVEL on the main screen, the TIME/BPM page or the chromatic menu (START/END/LEVEL blinks): CTRL 3 pans it from L50 through C to R50. In FWD mode the button combination stays Roland’s BWD.
  • Balance law: the far side goes down, nothing gets louder, so panning never clips.
  • Stereo and mono pads, from pads, MIDI, patterns and resampling, together with varispeed, Time Modify and chromatic mode. A mono pad’s new pan applies from its next start.

Saving and stock behaviour

  • Speeds, chromatic banks, mute groups and pans are saved on the card (VARISPD.BIN, CHROMA.BIN, MUTEGRP.BIN, PAN.BIN) and come back at power-on.
  • Speeds, mute groups and pans follow the sample: copy and exchange take them along; deleting, recording over or importing onto a pad resets them. A chromatic bank turns off if its source sample is deleted, recorded over or moved away.
  • Pads at normal speed / OFF, with no mute group and at pan C play bit-for-bit as with Roland’s firmware.
  • Roland’s factory hardware-test mode is removed to make room. Nothing else changes.
